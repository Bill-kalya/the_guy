## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Guy ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the The Guy mobile application, website, and related services (together, the "Platform").

By creating an account or using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of data as described in this Policy and in our Terms of Service.

## 2. Data We Collect

### Account and Profile Data

- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- National ID (Providers)
- Business permits (Providers, where applicable)
- Professional certifications (Providers, where applicable)
- Verification documents and verification status
- Profile photos and service images
- Ratings and reviews

### Usage and Device Data

- Device information (operating system, app version, device identifiers)
- App activity and interaction logs
- Payment references and transaction history

### Location Data

- Foreground location
- Background location during active jobs
- Live provider movement and route information
- Customer pickup locations

## 3. Location Tracking

The Guy collects and processes location data to operate the marketplace:

- To match Customers and Providers who are geographically nearby.
- To display nearby providers on the service discovery map.
- To provide real-time arrival tracking and ETA estimates for active jobs.
- To calculate distances for pricing and platform operations.

When the app is closed or not in use, we only collect location data if you have granted the appropriate permissions. You can disable location permissions at any time through your device settings; this may limit or prevent matching and tracking features.

## 4. How We Use Data

We use your data for:

- Matching customers and providers
- Job dispatch and fulfillment
- Route tracking and arrival estimates
- Fraud prevention and account security
- Reputation scoring and quality measurement
- Customer support
- Analytics and marketplace performance improvements
- Legal and regulatory compliance

## 5. Data Sharing

We may share your data with:

- Service providers and customers involved in a booking (for example, the provider assigned to your job and the customer who requested it)
- Payment processors (including M-Pesa and card processors)
- Verification and identity partners
- Cloud storage and infrastructure providers
- Law enforcement or regulators where legally required

We do not sell your personal data.

## 6. Data Retention and Security

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, for legal and accounting obligations, or as required by law.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

## 7. Kenyan Compliance

The Guy operates in compliance with:

- **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019**
- Applicable ICT and consumer protection regulations
- Other laws of Kenya that apply to personal data processing

We aim to comply with the data protection principles set out under Kenyan law, including lawful, fair, and transparent processing; purpose limitation; data minimization; and confidentiality and integrity of data.

## 8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request:

- Access to your personal data
- Correction of inaccurate data
- Deletion of your data where legally permitted
- Withdrawal of consent where applicable
- Restriction or objection to certain processing

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.
